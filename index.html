What to expect
  • fewer "yesterday" releases. files are shared by people who buy and rip CDs, then seed them. new drops take days or weeks to appear.
  • fewer russian releases. main userbase is western.
  • little to no soundcloud music. fresh web-only genres (hardtech, jumpstyle, slowed and reverb edits, TikTok mixes, YouTube rips) rarely exist here.
  • the search is less sensitive. an iTunes-api is being used to double-check song names, allowing for a margin for error in track names written by hand.
  • lossless is common. whole-album FLAC is often available from a single seeder. you are unlikely to hear a difference, depending on your audio setup.
  • some tracks take a few tries. it's a p2p network. go figure.
