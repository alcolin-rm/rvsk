What is Soulseek?

Soulseek is a peer-to-peer (P2P) file-sharing network, popular for music. When you download a file, you're downloading it directly from another user's computer, not from a central server. Likewise, other users can download files from you.

Because it's P2P:
  • You need a Soulseek account to use it.
  • Downloads go faster when more users share the same file.
  • Users who share files get faster download speeds themselves.
  • In some countries (e.g. Russia), the network may need a VPN to reach reliably.

Why only VK?

this tool reads track lists from vk playlists. spotify, deezer, soundcloud and the rest are all doable in theory. but in this case this app won't live long.

vk is free of western corporate overreach, 90% of the time. one track instance is taken down, 3 more appear.

if you are sour because your library is somewhere else, there are things you can do to bring it to vk. then come back here. but keep in mind that target audience are people who are already on vk.

Enter your Soulseek credentials

Your credentials are stored locally in sockseek.conf next to the app. Nothing is sent to any server.
